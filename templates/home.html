Watari

A web front end for rustypaste that gives every teammate their own login.

Why Watari?

rustypaste is a minimal self-hosted pastebin and file-sharing server. It only offers an HTTP API protected by shared tokens and has no notion of users, so everyone who wants to use it ends up holding the same raw token.

Watari sits in front of it. You sign in with your organization's SSO, and Watari calls rustypaste for you with a token picked from your groups. That token never reaches your browser, and rustypaste itself runs unchanged: shared links still point straight at it.

Features

  • Upload, paste, shorten: files, text and URLs, with optional expiry or one-shot links that disappear after the first view.
  • Password protection: content is encrypted in your browser (AES-GCM). The password and plaintext never reach the server; recipients unlock it on Watari's decrypt page without an account.
  • Single sign-on: OIDC login. Your groups decide which rustypaste token, and therefore which permissions, you get.
  • Dashboard: everything you've shared through Watari, with copy and delete.
